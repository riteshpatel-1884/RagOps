What it shows

C is the best option, and it has no reranker. Smaller chunks with plain vector search give the best any-hit and all-hit, and send 24% less text than v1.
The reranker doesn’t help. B improves a little but sends more text (4947 characters), because the reranker prefers long chunks. With 1000-character chunks it hurts all-hit (D 0.775 vs C 0.875), and it hurts it further at 700 characters (E 0.750). The tiny FlashRank model seems to push out the second piece of evidence that multi-hop questions need.
The gaps are small. 0.953 to 0.988 is about 3 questions out of 85. C is the best of what we tried, not a proven big win, and I picked it using the same questions it’s scored on. Treat the final judged numbers with that in mind.
